## Problems hit and how they were solved
- **Manim** couldn't be installed: pycairo needs Cairo headers and there was no sudo. Deferred (D4).
- **Port 8000** was occupied by another tool, so the API moved to 8787 (D11).
- A broad `pkill -f vite` was used once to stop the dev server; it could also have stopped unrelated Vite servers. Don't do this (see testing.md).
- **LaTeX bugs:** `\frac` inside a non-raw template literal became a form feed; `\sqrt{x}` written as JSX text made `{x}` a JS expression. Both are fixed, and `Tex` now logs invalid LaTeX in dev.
- **Mafs:** `Plot.OfX` connects across asymptotes and NaN (fixed with `FunctionPlot`); `<Text>` defaults to a huge font (now always sized); `contain` aspect made dense grids unreadable (`AutoGrid` uses fewer lines, with nearest 1/2/5 steps).
- **Mobile overflow:** responsive grids lacked `grid-cols-1`, and wide display math didn't scroll; both fixed.
- **Content errors** caught by checking the math: a car speed (15.6 → 12.6 m/s), where a sum's tangent is flat (x ≈ −1, not 1.9), and a symmetric-difference-quotient claim at an inflection point.
- **Test-selector trap:** `text=Graph it` matched the prose "graph it is…".
